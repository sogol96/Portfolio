About

Hello, I'm Sogol.

I'm a graphic designer and illustrator. For six years I've made visuals for board games, apps and brands, and I draw my own ink worlds alongside. At Asanbar, I was part of the marketing team that turned social content into results: in nine months, followers grew 33%, engagement reached 8% (most brands see 2–3%), likes and comments grew tenfold, and sales tripled. I'd like to bring that mix of drawing and data to a game, publishing or brand team.
